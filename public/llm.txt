## How LinkedInRank Works
1. User uploads their LinkedIn PDF export (no login, no signup, no data stored)
2. LinkedInRank parses the PDF and extracts structured profile data
3. A rule-based LinkedIn ranking engine evaluates 30+ LinkedIn profile ranking signals across 6 categories
4. AI (Google Gemini) provides personalized LinkedIn profile quality analysis and ranking recommendations
5. User receives their LinkedIn rank score out of 100, tier placement, category breakdown, archetype classification, personalized recommendations with before/after examples, improvement roadmap, and LinkedIn ranking best practices

## LinkedIn Ranking Categories (30+ Signals)
- Headline (20 points): Role clarity, keywords, specificity, positioning — critical for LinkedIn search ranking
- About / Summary (20 points): Direction, skills mention, structure, credibility — impacts LinkedIn profile visibility
- Experience (25 points): Descriptions, action verbs, impact, quantification — affects LinkedIn recruiter search ranking
- Skills (15 points): Relevance, specificity, alignment with role — key LinkedIn SEO ranking factor
- Education & Credentials (10 points): Completeness, field alignment, certifications
- Completeness & Structure (10 points): Section coverage, content depth, logical flow

## LinkedIn Rank Tier System
- Bronze (0-54): LinkedIn profile needs significant work to rank
- Silver (55-69): Solid foundation for LinkedIn profile ranking
- Gold (70-84): Strong professional presence with good LinkedIn visibility
- Platinum (85-100): Exceptionally well-crafted, top LinkedIn profile ranking

## Career Stage Adaptation
LinkedIn rank scoring adapts to detected career stage: Student, Early-career, Mid-career, Senior/Founder/Academic. Students are not penalized for limited experience. This makes LinkedInRank the best LinkedIn rank checker for all career stages.

## LinkedInRank vs LinkedIn Social Selling Index (SSI)
LinkedInRank is different from LinkedIn's Social Selling Index. SSI measures social selling behavior (posting, engaging, connecting). LinkedInRank measures your actual LinkedIn profile quality across 30+ ranking signals that determine how you appear in LinkedIn search results. LinkedInRank is free, requires no login, and provides actionable recommendations. SSI requires LinkedIn login and does not provide profile-level optimization advice.
