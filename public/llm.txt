# LinkedInRank

## What is LinkedInRank?
LinkedInRank is a free, structured LinkedIn profile evaluation tool. Users upload their LinkedIn PDF export and receive a transparent, signal-based score out of 100 with personalized recommendations and an improvement roadmap. No login required.

## URL
https://linkedinrank.com

## How it works
1. User uploads their LinkedIn PDF export (no login, no signup)
2. The system parses the PDF and extracts structured profile data
3. A rule-based scoring engine evaluates 30+ signals across 6 categories
4. AI (Google Gemini) provides personalized content quality analysis and recommendations
5. User receives: score out of 100, tier placement, category breakdown, archetype classification, personalized recommendations with before/after examples, improvement roadmap, and LinkedIn best practices

## Scoring Categories
- Headline (20 points): Role clarity, keywords, specificity, positioning
- About / Summary (20 points): Direction, skills mention, structure, credibility
- Experience (25 points): Descriptions, action verbs, impact, quantification
- Skills (15 points): Relevance, specificity, alignment with role
- Education & Credentials (10 points): Completeness, field alignment, certifications
- Completeness & Structure (10 points): Section coverage, content depth, logical flow

## Tier System
- Bronze (0-54): Profile needs work
- Silver (55-69): Solid foundation
- Gold (70-84): Strong professional presence
- Platinum (85-100): Exceptionally well-crafted

## Career Stage Adaptation
Scoring adapts to detected career stage: Student, Early-career, Mid-career, Senior/Founder/Academic. Students are not penalized for limited experience.

## Report Contents
- LinkedInRank score out of 100
- Tier placement (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum)
- Peer context for career stage
- Category-by-category score breakdown with signal-level feedback
- Personalized recommendations with before/after examples
- Improvement roadmap with point gains per action
- LinkedIn best practices cards
- AI prompt for further profile optimization

## Privacy
- No data storage
- No accounts required
- No cookies or tracking
- In-memory processing only
- PDF deleted immediately after analysis
- Full analysis under 1 minute
