## What Is LinkedInRank?
LinkedInRank is a free LinkedIn profile rank checker and scoring tool. Upload your LinkedIn PDF and get an instant LinkedIn rank score out of 100 across 30+ LinkedIn ranking signals. No login required. No data stored.

## Core Purpose
LinkedInRank helps professionals understand exactly how their LinkedIn profile performs in recruiter searches. It scores your profile across 30+ ranking signals — headline keywords, about section quality, completeness, engagement signals, skills optimization, and more — and gives AI-powered, actionable recommendations to improve your LinkedIn rank and get noticed by recruiters.

## LinkedIn Rank Score Tiers
- Bronze: 0–39 — profile has critical gaps reducing LinkedIn visibility
- Silver: 40–59 — profile has a foundation but needs keyword and content improvements
- Gold: 60–79 — solid LinkedIn profile with strong ranking signals
- Platinum: 80–100 — top-tier LinkedIn profile with maximum recruiter visibility

## LinkedIn Rank Scoring Methodology
LinkedInRank uses a 30+ signal scoring engine that weights signals by their actual impact on LinkedIn recruiter search ranking. The most important signals are: headline keyword optimization (weighted highest), profile completeness to All-Star level, keyword placement in headline and about section, skill endorsements from 3+ connections, and connection count above 500. Signals are scored individually, combined into category scores (Profile Visibility, Content Quality, Keyword SEO, Network Strength, Engagement), and then weighted into a composite rank out of 100.

## LinkedIn Rank Scoring — Career Stage Adaptation
LinkedIn rank scoring adapts to detected career stage: Student, Early-career, Mid-career, Senior/Founder/Academic. Students are not penalized for limited experience. This makes LinkedInRank the best LinkedIn rank checker for all career stages.

## LinkedInRank vs LinkedIn Social Selling Index (SSI)
LinkedInRank is different from LinkedIn's Social Selling Index. SSI measures social selling behavior (posting, engaging, connecting). LinkedInRank measures your actual LinkedIn profile quality across 30+ ranking signals that determine how you appear in LinkedIn search results. LinkedInRank is free, requires no login, and provides actionable recommendations. SSI requires LinkedIn login and does not provide profile-level optimization advice.
